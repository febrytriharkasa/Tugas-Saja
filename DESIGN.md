## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a prestigious, contemporary educational identity for an elite Islamic senior high school. It blends spiritual rootedness with academic excellence, digital-first accessibility, and institutional transparency. The visual language speaks directly to prospective students, parents, alumni, and faculty, evoking trust, progressive innovation, and moral grounding.

Drawing from modern high-performance institutional design and clean SaaS ergonomics, the style combines:
- Crisp white canvas foundations accented by deep emerald green grounding surfaces.
- Fresh, optimistic cyan and bright teal highlights for interactive touchpoints, dynamic states, and student-focused updates.
- Subtle warm gold/amber accents for achievement, Islamic ethos, and ceremonial honors.
- High-contrast typography with generous breathing space, soft-diffused elevation, and rounded architectural elements that deliver an approachable yet prestigious academic atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette balances classical Islamic heritage with modern digital clarity:
- **Primary Emerald (`#065F46` / `#047857`)**: Anchors institutional authority, leadership messages, top navigation banners, and primary calls to action.
- **Secondary Cyan/Teal (`#0891B2` / `#06B6D4`)**: Energizes category chips, quick links, event timestamps, progress bars, and hover indicators.
- **Tertiary Amber/Gold (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`)**: Reserved for academic awards, accreditation markers, badges of honor, and key notifications.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`)**: Uses Slate-inspired neutral gradations. Crisp pure white `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, `#F8FAFC` for background canvas layers, `#E2E8F0` for hairline dividers, and `#334155` / `#0F172A` for high-legibility typographic hierarchies.

## Typography

The design system exclusively leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all tiers to deliver a clean, geometric, yet humane warmth fitting for modern pedagogy. Section headers employ dual-colored composition (e.g., strong slate for the subject noun paired with vivid cyan for the descriptor) to establish rhythm. 

- Uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em`) is selectively applied to metadata badges, author tags, and academic stream labels.
- Hero and heading levels utilize tight negative letter-spacing for commanding impact.
- Body text remains uncompressed and relaxed to ensure effortless scanning of institutional circulars, curriculum details, and news releases.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column layout handles desktop views (max container width: 1280px), transitioning into a 6-column grid on tablets, and a unified 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>1024px)**: Outer margins set to `margin-lg` (`4rem`) with a `gutter-lg` (`2rem`). Hero split layouts and news matrices utilize 3-to-4 column spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Outer margins adjust to `margin-md` (`2rem`) with cards reflowing into a balanced 2-column pattern.
- **Mobile (<767px)**: Outer canvas margin tightens to `margin` (`1rem`) with horizontal scroll carousels for partner logos, quick-action cards, and staff rosters.
- Generous inter-section vertical separation (`4rem` to `6rem`) ensures clear division between admission portals, daily news feeds, teacher profiles, and announcements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach of ambient colored shadows and subtle low-contrast boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Pure `#F8FAFC` base surface without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Container Resting)**: Solid white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a subtle 1px border (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`), accompanied by an ambient drop-shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Quick Portals)**: Elevated upon user interaction. Shadow transitions to `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.08)` with a `-2px` transform on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Navigation Headers & Overlays)**: Backdropped translucent header with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` fill, 12px blur filter, and a base edge border of `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.
- **Badge & Pill Elements**: Pure flat surfaces with solid soft-tone background fills, avoiding heavy internal shadows to maintain clarity.

## Shapes

The roundedness language is calibrated to Level 2 (Rounded - `0.5rem` base, `1rem` on cards, `1.5rem` on prominent features):
- **Cards, Panels, and Featured Images**: Implement `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) to soften photographic content and framing borders.
- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields**: Configured with `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) for structured, confident touch areas.
- **Status Tags, Date Badges, and Pill Chips**: Utilize full pill roundedness (`9999px`) to offset rectangular grid cards, producing a distinct visual distinction for categorical data.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid emerald green `#065F46` fill, white `#FFFFFF` label, subtle emerald hover `#047857`. Medium size has `0.625rem` vertical and `1.25rem` horizontal padding with `rounded-lg`.
- **Secondary / Action Teal**: Solid `#0891B2` fill with white text, used for quick admission portals, library access, and e-learning portals.
- **Ghost / Text**: Deep emerald label with an arrow icon (`Selengkapnya →`), transparent background, displaying a faint teal underline upon hover.

### Cards
- **News / Article Card**: Vertical layout with an overflow-hidden 16:9 thumbnail, `rounded-xl` border radius, subtle border outline, category chip placed above the title, and secondary metadata (reporter, timestamp) anchored at the bottom.
- **Staff / Teacher Card**: Portrait framing with top-rounded photo container, white biographical pad containing name in bold slate, subject tag, and unified contact action icons (phone, email, message) centered in emerald circles.
- **Quick Service Portal**: Horizontal interactive card featuring a colored container icon (e.g., student registration, mobile app, library), bold title, and inline action button.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Category Tags**: Fully rounded (`9999px`), `0.25rem` vertical by `0.75rem` horizontal padding. Tinted cyan background (`#ECFEFF`) with high-contrast text (`#0E7490`), or light emerald (`#ECFDF5`) with deep emerald text (`#065F46`).
- **Date Block Badges**: Stacked vertical rounded rectangle for calendar agendas (top section dark slate with month abbreviation; bottom section bright cyan with large bold date digits).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Text Inputs**: White container, 1px slate border (`#CBD5E1`), placeholder in `#94A3B8`, transitioning on focus to an active emerald boundary (`#059669`) with a 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Standard rounded or circular toggles filled with deep emerald on selection, with crisp white glyphs.

### Additional Educational Components
- **Value Proposition Bar**: High-density horizontal feature bar displaying core pillars (e.g., *Berakhlak Mulia*, *Inovasi & Teknologi*) with muted line icons, positioned below the primary hero section.
- **Institutional Header**: Two-tier structure with a top utility bar (contact info, PPDB links, language switcher in dark slate `#0F172A`) and a clean white primary navigation bar featuring the school crest, streamlined link items, and search trigger.
